Create a clean, professional infographic poster comparing 4 types of part-time jobs in Chinese. Use a comparison-matrix or card-based layout.

The 4 job types to compare:

1. 日结工 (Daily Paid)
   - Settlement: Same day
   - Pros: Fast cash, best for urgent needs
   - Cons: No long-term security, low stability
   - Best for: People needing urgent cash, flexible workers

2. 周结工 (Weekly Paid)
   - Settlement: Weekly
   - Pros: Better stability, predictable income
   - Cons: Need to wait one week
   - Best for: Those needing stable cash flow, flexible schedule

3. 月结工 (Monthly Paid)
   - Settlement: End of month
   - Pros: Good for long-term planning, similar to formal employment
   - Cons: Need short-term savings to bridge gap
   - Best for: College students, transition job seekers

4. 寒暑假工 (Seasonal Vacation Work)
   - Settlement: After work period ends
   - Pros: Better pay, full work experience
   - Cons: Highly seasonal, limited opportunities
   - Best for: Students, industry-specific workers
