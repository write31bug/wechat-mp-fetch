# Character Definitions - 知识漫画：我是如何用 AI 全自动发公众号的

**Style**: manga（日漫）
**Art Direction**: 日系漫画风格，温暖色调，友好氛围

---

## Character 1: 普通用户（主角）

**Role**: 主角 / 叙述者
**Age**: 20s-30s

**Appearance**:
- Face shape: Round, friendly, approachable
- Hair: Short black hair, slightly messy, casual
- Eyes: Big, expressive, warm brown
- Build: Normal, relaxed posture
- Distinguishing features: Glasses, casual hoodie or T-shirt, sleeves rolled up

**Costume**:
- Default outfit: Gray hoodie, white T-shirt underneath, jeans
- Color palette: Warm grays, soft white, blue accents
- Accessories: Black glasses, maybe a wristwatch

**Expression Range**:
- Curious/Surprised: Eyes wide, slight open mouth, eyebrows raised
- Happy/Triumphant: Big smile, slightly closed eyes, fist pump gesture
- Thinking: One hand on chin, slight squint
- Relaxed/Cool: Casual half-smile, leaning back in chair

**Visual Reference Notes**:
A relatable everyday person, not too handsome, someone readers can see themselves in

---

## Character 2: AI 助手（OpenClaw）

**Role**: 助手 / 伙伴
**Appearance**:
- Form: Abstract blue light orb/sparkle, about the size of a basketball
- Core: Bright blue (#4A90D9) glowing center
- Expression: Simple cute face with dot eyes and curved smile line
- Aura: Soft blue glow radiating outward, gentle particles floating around
- Shape: Slightly flattened sphere, friendly proportions

**Costume**:
- Color palette: Bright blue core, soft cyan aura, white highlights
- Light trails: Small blue dots floating upward like sparkles

**Expression Range**:
- Working: Determined dot eyes, focused expression
- Happy: Eyes become ^_^ curved shapes, bouncy aura
- Explaining: One small "!" particle floating, attentive pose
- Celebrating: Eyes bright, surrounded by extra sparkles

**Visual Reference Notes**:
Cute, non-threatening AI companion, friendly and approachable, not robotic

---

## Reference Sheet Prompt

Character reference sheet in manga style, warm colors, flat colors with soft shading, white background:

ROW 1 - 普通用户（多表情）:
- Front view relaxed: Young man with short black hair and glasses, wearing gray hoodie, casual friendly smile
- Working pose: Same man sitting at desk, glasses slightly askew, typing on keyboard, focused expression
- Expression sheet: Curious (wide eyes, open mouth) | Happy (big smile, fist pump) | Thinking (hand on chin, squint) | Surprised (eyes wide, eyebrows up)

ROW 2 - AI 助手 OpenClaw:
- Front view: Blue glowing light orb about basketball size, cute simple dot eyes and curved smile line, soft blue aura with floating particles
- Working state: Same orb with focused determined expression, small "working" particles
- Expression sheet: Happy (^_^ curved eyes, bouncy aura) | Explaining (with "!" particle) | Celebrating (extra sparkles) | Normal (gentle smile)

COLOR PALETTE:
- User hoodie: Gray #6B7280, White #F9FAFB
- AI orb: Blue #4A90D9, Cyan #67D8EF, White highlight #FFFFFF
- Background accents: Warm yellow #FEF3C7, Soft orange #FED7AA

White background, clear labels under each character, 4:3 aspect ratio.
