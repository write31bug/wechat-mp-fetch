## Character 1: 总统（主角）

**Role**: 主角 / 叙述者
**Age**: 70s

**Appearance**:
- Face shape: Oval, angular jaw, prominent chin
- Hair: Silver-blond, swoopy combed style, distinct hairline
- Eyes: Blue-gray, intense expression, heavy eyelids
- Build: Tall, imposing, carries himself with authority
- Distinguishing features: Orange skin tone (heavily tanned/full makeup), wide shoulders in suits

**Costume**:
- Inauguration: Dark suit, red tie, American flag pin, very crisp and sharp
- Fallen state: Same suit but rumpled, tie loosened, tired posture
- Color palette: Navy suit (#1A365D), red tie (#E53E3E), gold accessories (#ECC94B)

**Expression Range**:
- Proud/Arrogant: Chin up, slight smirk, wide stance, eyes half-closed
- Angry/Wronged: Red face, furrowed brows, pointing finger
- Desperate: Wide eyes, sweat drops, trembling hands
- Humble/Pleading: Softened expression, clasped hands, hopeful look

**Visual Reference Notes**:
Based on public figures, stylized caricature for dramatic storytelling
