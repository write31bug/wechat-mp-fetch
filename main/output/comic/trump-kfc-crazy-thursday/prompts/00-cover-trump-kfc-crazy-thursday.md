# 封面页绘图 Prompt

## Prompt

Ligne Claire comic cover page in dramatic cinematic style, 16:9 aspect ratio. Epic inauguration scene on white house steps. A silver-blond man in his 70s with orange-tanned skin, wearing a crisp navy suit with red tie and American flag pin, stands triumphantly on top of tall steps. Behind him looms a massive border wall stretching upward, American flags on either side. Below him, a dark crowd raises hands cheering. Dramatic golden-hour lighting with spotlight effect on the protagonist. Clean uniform lines (2px), flat colors, no gradients. High contrast between golden highlights and deep shadows. Chinese caption at bottom: "我本是美利坚的总统，加冕称王那天……". Watermark "日食三餐" in bottom-right corner, opacity 0.5.
