Abstract
Fungi, such as Candida spp., are commonly found on the skin and at mucosal surfaces. Yet, they rarely cause invasive
infectionsinimmunocompetentindividuals,an observationreflectingtheabilityofourinnateimmunesystemtocontrol
potentially invasive microbes found at biological boundaries. Antimicrobial proteins and peptides are becoming
increasingly recognized as important effectors of innate immunity. This is illustrated further by the present investigation,
demonstratinganovelantifungalroleofhistidine-richglycoprotein(HRG),anabundantandmultimodularplasmaprotein.
