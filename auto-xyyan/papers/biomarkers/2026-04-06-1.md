## Key Findings
Research on sepsis prediction models has evolved from traditional scoring systems to machine learning and deep learning approaches capable of integrating large-scale, multimodal clinical data. This has given rise to numerous sepsis prediction models based on machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL), which demonstrate distinct strengths in discrimination under appropriate data availability and validation settings. Major methodological paradigms include rule-based clinical scores, conventional machine learning, deep learning with temporal modeling, and emerging multimodal approaches.

## Clinical Significance
Machine learning models for sepsis prediction offer enhanced capability to integrate large-scale, multimodal clinical data for earlier detection and intervention, though they introduce higher requirements for data quality, implementation, and reproducibility compared to traditional clinical scores.
