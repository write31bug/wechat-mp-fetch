## Key Findings
This review synthesizes the current understanding of sepsis biomarkers, focusing on their utility in delineating the intricate pathophysiological mechanisms of sepsis. The paper covers recent advances in sepsis biomarkers and explores future perspectives for their clinical application in diagnosis, prognosis, and therapeutic monitoring.

## Clinical Significance
Provides comprehensive overview of current and emerging sepsis biomarkers that could improve early diagnosis, risk stratification, and treatment monitoring in septic patients.
