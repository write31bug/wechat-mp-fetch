## Key Findings
Experts addressed upcoming guidelines for hemodynamic monitoring in adult patients with sepsis and septic shock. Focus areas include evidence-based use of four specific hemodynamic monitoring themes: fluid responsiveness, invasive vs noninvasive blood pressure monitoring, target mean arterial pressure (MAP) for resuscitation, and invasive central venous pressure (CVP)-guided therapy. Studies comparing invasive and noninvasive blood pressure monitoring recommend early invasive arterial blood pressure for septic shock monitoring. Large-scale studies found that noninvasive blood pressure monitoring techniques clinically and significantly underestimated MAP readings in septic shock patients.

## Clinical Significance
Provides insight into upcoming evidence-based guidelines for hemodynamic monitoring in sepsis that will inform optimal monitoring strategies and resuscitation targets for patients with septic shock.
