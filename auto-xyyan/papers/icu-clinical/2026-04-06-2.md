## Key Findings
Point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) is a non-invasive, real-time, bedside imaging modality that has been widely implemented in septic patients due to its versatility, repeatability, and ability to provide immediate diagnostic and monitoring information. POCUS enables rapid assessment of cardiac function, volume status, and organ perfusion in septic patients, facilitating timely therapeutic interventions.

## Clinical Significance
POCUS serves as a valuable tool for real-time assessment and monitoring of septic patients at the bedside, enabling rapid diagnostic evaluation and guiding therapeutic decisions in critical care settings.
